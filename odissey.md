# 🧭 **The Odyssey — Five Eras**

## 🚀 Era I — Startup & Proto‑Streaming (1999 → y2k)
I began in late 1999 inside a to‑IPO startup delivering **MP3/RTSP music** over the Internet and early mobile channels (GPRS/WAP), long before “streaming” became mainstream.  
With a strong background in **software programming & design** (jdk1.x, patterns, J2EE), I embraced PHP, Perl, and open-source tooling to build a **24×7 production-grade platform** on Linux/Solaris.

Highlights:

- early mobile streaming  
- Perl/regex pipelines  
- distributed caching & monitoring (SNMP/JMX)  
- XML‑HTTP integrations with Sony/Vivendi/Universal/Nokia
